
It should be a website/ app that provides all the information needed to land a job. This isn’t just a website or app. Its primary purpose isn’t to require users to log in and then go to LeetCode to practice; instead, it should offer an easy, welcoming experience.

Why do we need this?  

Previously, software engineering was relatively straightforward, perhaps because interview preparation was simple. Materials were scarce online, but now the internet is bombarded with information from many sources. The content is scattered, making it hard to find reliable advice amid the noise.  

The main idea is to consolidate a single artifact that helps AI retrieve the correct information. This page should present the right way to prepare based on the gathered data and ready candidates for their next role. As roles evolve, we can refine our artifacts and generate enough intel for users to upgrade themselves without doing the hard work or without getting lost
